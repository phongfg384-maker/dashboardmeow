1. Overview

If you believe content associated with the Bot or dashboard infringes your copyright, you may submit a copyright complaint.

2. Required Information

Please include your full legal name, contact email, description of the copyrighted work, the exact location of the allegedly infringing content, and a statement that you believe the report is accurate.

3. Good-Faith Reporting

Only submit claims in good faith.

False or abusive reports may be rejected or ignored.

4. Counter Notification

If you believe content was removed or restricted in error, you may send a counter notice with the information reasonably needed to review your request.

5. Repeat Infringers

Accounts or servers that repeatedly submit valid infringement complaints or abuse the process may be restricted.

6. Review Process

We may review reports and take reasonable actions such as removal, restriction, or request for more information.

7. Contact

Send copyright requests through the support server or official contact points listed on the dashboard.
