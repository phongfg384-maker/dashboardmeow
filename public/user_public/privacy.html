1. Information We Collect

We may store guild IDs, user IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, configuration values, logs, economy data, ticket data, and other operational records required for the Bot to work.

If AI features are enabled, prompts and responses may be temporarily processed for the requested feature.

2. How We Use Data

Data is used to deliver Bot features, remember server settings, provide moderation and logging, and prevent abuse.

We may also use data to troubleshoot errors and improve the service.

3. Data Storage

Data may be stored in databases, log files, or third-party services used to run the Bot.

We take reasonable steps to protect stored data, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data.

Data may be shared only when required by law, to protect the service, or to operate services provided by trusted vendors.

5. Data Retention

We keep data only as long as needed for the service or as required by law, security, or dispute handling.

When practical, server owners may request deletion of eligible server data.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to certain processing of your data.

Requests can usually be made through the support server or dashboard contact options.

7. Third-Party Services

The Bot may rely on Discord API, hosting providers, database providers, AI vendors, and other tools needed to operate.

Those third parties have their own privacy terms and policies.

8. Children's Privacy

The Bot is intended for users who are allowed to use Discord according to Discord's age rules and the laws that apply to them.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may change over time.

The updated version becomes effective when posted.

10. Contact

If you have questions about privacy, data removal, or retention, contact the support server.
